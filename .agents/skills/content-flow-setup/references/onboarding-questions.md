# Focused onboarding questions

Select only the next highest-value question. Ask one at a time.

## Identity and purpose

- What work or lived experience gives you standing to write about your main topics?
- Who specifically should find your posts useful, and what should change for them?
- Which two or three topics do you want to return to—and which are off-limits?
- Is your goal to teach, provoke discussion, document learning, attract opportunities, or something else?

## Voice and taste

- Share two pieces that sound like you and one that does not; what distinction matters to you?
- Do you prefer direct claims, stories, questions, or another opening pattern?
- What writing habits make a post feel generic, inflated, or unlike you?
- How comfortable are you with vulnerability, disagreement, humor, jargon, and strong claims?

## Evidence and boundaries

- Which sources or source types do you trust, and which require extra skepticism?
- How should links or citations appear in a LinkedIn post?
- What client, employer, personal, or confidential details must never be included?
- Which claims always need your personal fact-check or legal/compliance review?

## Format

- What post length and paragraph rhythm feel natural?
- Do you use lists, emojis, hashtags, calls to action, or links? State “not established” when undecided.
- What should a strong ending accomplish for your audience?
