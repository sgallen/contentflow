# Content brief

- **Audience:** Managers of small product and engineering teams.
- **Thesis:** A short decision log is valuable when it prevents a team from paying twice to reconstruct an expensive choice.
- **Concrete example:** Six people reopened synchronous invoice generation; the choice held after they rebuilt the old reasoning.
- **Causal detail:** Four fields preserve the choice, reason, owner, and explicit revisit trigger.
- **Tension:** Documentation can become bureaucracy; logging every choice defeats the purpose.
- **Payoff:** Run a two-week test on choices expensive to reconstruct, then keep or stop based on use.
- **Evidence boundary:** Internal fictional case only; omit the unmeasured “saved a week” claim.
- **Confidentiality:** Use fictional project context without customer/security detail.
