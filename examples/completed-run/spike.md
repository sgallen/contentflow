# Decision logs are latency tools, not documentation

> Fictional example: organizations, people, data, and source packet below are invented for demonstration.

## Idea

Small product teams should use short decision logs because they reduce the time spent reopening settled choices—not because documentation is inherently virtuous.

## Why it may be worth developing

The framing connects a familiar practice to delivery speed and gives managers a testable behavior.

## Original source or provenance

Creator's retrospective after the fictional Northstar Billing migration at Acorn Studio.

## Known assumptions

- Repeated debate was caused partly by lost decision context.
- A lightweight log changed that behavior rather than merely coinciding with it.

## Unresolved questions
