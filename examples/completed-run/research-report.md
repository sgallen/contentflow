# Research report

## Scope and source note

Completed 2026-07-10 against a **fictional local source packet** for this example. Links use the reserved `.invalid` domain and are intentionally non-live.

## Verified facts within the fictional packet

- Three architecture questions were reopened across six meetings. Source: [sanitized retrospective](https://acorn.example.invalid/northstar-retro), sections 2–3.
- Meeting notes estimate 14 participant-hours spent reconstructing prior reasoning. Source: [meeting audit](https://acorn.example.invalid/meeting-audit), table 1.
- The team adopted a four-field log on 2026-05-04. Source: [decision-log change](https://acorn.example.invalid/change-104).

## Disputed claims

- “The log saved a week” is a participant estimate, not measured elapsed time.
- One engineer attributed improvement partly to a smaller meeting group.

## Unresolved claims

- None blocking. The numerical time estimate remains disputed and is excluded from the post.

## Interpretation

The packet supports a bounded claim that recoverable reasoning reduced repeated discussion for this team. It does not prove that decision logs universally increase delivery speed.

## Useful interview questions

- Reconstruct one reopened decision and its consequence.
- Why did the log work when ordinary meeting notes did not?
- What is the strongest bureaucracy objection?
- What should a reader try next week?
