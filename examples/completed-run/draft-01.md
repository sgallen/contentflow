## Main body

During a billing migration, we reopened whether invoices should be generated synchronously.

Six people spent most of a meeting reconstructing constraints we'd already discussed. We reached the same answer.

So we created a decision log. It captured the decision, rationale, owner and date. This created alignment and a single source of truth. Teams move faster when knowledge is documented.

The lesson is simple: write down important decisions. This prevents wasted meetings and improves velocity.
