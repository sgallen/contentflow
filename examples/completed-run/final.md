## Approved post

Decision logs aren't documentation. They're a latency tool.

During a billing migration, six of us reopened whether invoices should be generated synchronously.

We spent most of a meeting reconstructing constraints we'd already discussed—and reached the same answer.

We had paid twice for one decision.

Our fix wasn't detailed minutes. For choices expensive to reconstruct, we recorded four things:

- decision
- reason
- owner
- revisit trigger

That last field mattered. It didn't forbid disagreement. It said what would need to change before we debated again.

Try this for two weeks. Log only the decisions that would be costly to reconstruct. If nobody reopens one or uses a revisit trigger, stop.

The goal isn't a complete archive. It's less time rebuilding the path to a choice you've already made.
