# LinkedIn format guidance

## Stable requirements

- Produce three hook options, one substantive body, three closing options, and a recommended assembly during drafting.
- Record a deterministic Unicode character count for the assembled and final versions.
- Favor readable paragraphing and a clear payoff appropriate to the intended audience.
- Flag links, citations, factual caveats, and confidential details for human review.
